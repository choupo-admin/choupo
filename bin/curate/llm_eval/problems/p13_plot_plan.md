# Problem 13
A feed of 100 kmol/h of ethanol and water (20 mol % ethanol) at 25 C and 1.5 bar is heated by 150 kW in a heater and then flashed at 1.013 bar in a flash drum. Size the heater as a shell-and-tube exchanger against low-pressure steam (U = 800 W/m2K, design pressure 4 bar) and the drum as a 5 m3 vessel (length/diameter 3, design pressure 3 bar), both in stainless steel 316. Then make the plot plan of the plant: the heater (a box 6 m x 1 m x 1.5 m) at the origin and the drum 10 m east of it, a single pipe rack 5 m north of them (6 m high, 4 lanes) running from 15 m west to 25 m east of the origin, a control room (10 m x 6 m x 4 m, an ignition source) 30 m south of the drum, a site gate at the south-west and a 6 m wide road from the gate to the control room. The wind blows from the west and the control room must stay at least 15 m (edge to edge) from the vessel. Report the total pipe length, the plot area (use a 5 m margin) and whether the layout is accepted or announces any finding.
