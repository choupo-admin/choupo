# Problem 07
A gas of 1000 kmol/h at 30 C and 10 bar with 8 mol% CO2 in nitrogen is contacted counter-currently with 400 kmol/h of pure liquid water at 30 C and 10 bar in a plate absorber. Find the CO2 removal fraction, the CO2 content of the leaving gas, and the CO2 loading of the leaving water, and state how many equilibrium stages you used.
