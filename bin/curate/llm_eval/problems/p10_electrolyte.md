# Problem 10
A brine of 1000 kg/h with 3.5 wt% NaCl in water at 25 C and 1 bar is evaporated in a single-stage flash at 60 C and 0.1 bar. Report the vapour flow, the concentrated brine flow and its NaCl concentration (wt%), and the heat duty. The salt does not volatilise, and the boiling-point elevation and water activity of the brine must be accounted for.
