# Problem 08
Air-like gas (79 mol% N2, 21 mol% O2), 500 kmol/h at 1 bar and 25 C, must be delivered at 16 bar. Use two compressor stages with equal pressure ratio, each with isentropic efficiency 0.75, and cool the gas to 40 C after each stage. Report the discharge temperature of each stage, the shaft work of each stage, and the total cooling duty.
