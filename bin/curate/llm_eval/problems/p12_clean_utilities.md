# Problem 12
A three-room oral-solid-dose tablet suite sits in a single-storey building (floor height 4 m): a blending room (8 m x 5 m), a pressing room (6 m x 5 m) and a packing room (6 m x 5 m) side by side, all opening on a corridor 20 m x 2 m along their south wall, and a plant room (20 m x 5 m) south of the corridor that houses the air-handling units and is not itself conditioned. The three rooms and the corridor are cleanliness grade D, the plant room is controlled-not-classified. The material balance is a granulating-water stream of 10 kg/h mixed with 100 kg/h of lactose powder into a wet blend (the only process unit), and the plant makes a lot of 50 kg of wet blend, 405 lots a year in one train of 3 840 h with campaigns of 8 lots and 24 h changeover; the wet-blend stage takes 0.5 h to charge, 1 h to process, 0.25 h to discharge and 1 h to clean. Size the clean utilities: the HVAC with 20 air changes per hour in grade D rooms and 6 per hour in the controlled-not-classified room, a fan of 1 000 Pa pressure rise at 60 percent efficiency, 20 percent fresh air, supply at 20 C, outdoor design temperatures 2 C in winter and 32 C in summer; and the purified water: the granulating water above is drawn over 0.5 h at the start of the wet-blend stage, and 0.5 m3 of purified water per lot is used for cleaning at the end of that stage; the generator is rated 25 percent above the peak draw and the storage tank holds 4 h of peak draw. Report the supply air flow, the fan power, the winter heating and summer cooling duties, the peak purified-water draw, the generator capacity and the tank volume.
