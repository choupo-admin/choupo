# Problem 02
A stream of 50 kmol/h, 40 mol% ethanol and 60 mol% water, at 25 C and 1.013 bar is fed to a drum held at 78 C and 1.013 bar. Find the vapour and liquid flows and compositions, and the heat duty needed to reach that state. The mixture is strongly non-ideal and forms an azeotrope; the result must reflect that.
