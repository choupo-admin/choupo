# Problem 11
A small oral-solid-dose tablet line makes uncoated tablets of 0.5 g each from a blend of three powders: 98.0 wt % lactose monohydrate (excipient), 1.0 wt % of an active ingredient (take C20H25N3O, molar mass 323.4 kg/kmol, as a stand-in, density 1300 kg/m3) and 1.0 wt % magnesium stearate (lubricant), all fed at 25 C and 1 atm as solids. The blend is pressed into tablets on a rotary press (1 percent of the pressed tablets are rejected by weight checks) and the good tablets are packed in blisters of 28 tablets. The steady flowsheet is the material balance at 100 kg/h of blend. Plan the plant in lots: the lot is 50 kg of blend (one blender load), the plant must deliver 25 000 kg of packed tablets a year, one train runs 3 840 h a year in campaigns of 8 lots with 24 h changeover. Cycle times per lot: blender charge 0.5 h, blend 0.5 h, discharge 0.25 h, clean 1 h (bulk density 500 kg/m3, 60 percent filled); press set-up 0.5 h, run 1.5 h, clean 1 h; blister line set-up 0.5 h, run 2 h, clean 0.5 h. The machines (assumed vendor ratings): a bin blender of 0.2 m3 nominal volume filled to at most 60 percent; a rotary press with 27 stations, 50 rpm, one compression per revolution; a blister line at 40 blisters per minute. Report the lots a year, the limiting cycle time and the train occupancy, and the utilisation of the blender, the press and the blister line (say whether each machine fits the lot).
