# Problem 03
A feed of 200 kmol/h of 30 mol% propane, 40 mol% n-butane and 30 mol% n-pentane at 25 C and 8 bar is heated and then flashed at 8 bar. The flash must deliver exactly 50 mol% of the feed as vapour. Determine the temperature the heater must reach, the heat duty, and the compositions of the vapour and liquid products.
