# Third pass (branch `build/c335-pins`): the two pins, measured; nothing promoted

## (a) SRK N2-CH4: conflict between two uses of ONE primary, not a compilation anchor

flash09_n2ch4_stryjek's anchor (K_N2 3.047, K_CH4 0.638, band 3 %) is Stryjek,
Chappelear & Kobayashi 1974 Table III itself, the 149.82 K / 20.616 bar point
(x_N2 0.1503, y_N2 0.4579, so K_N2 = 3.047), a measurement from a primary paper.
It is not the compilation the record 0.0289 came from.  The earlier fit of kij to
the same Table III (all isotherms, 8 isotherms 122-183 K) gives 0.0058 and leaves
this very point at K_N2 2.864 (-6 %).  So the primary's single isotherm and the
primary's global fit disagree through the cubic: kij is temperature dependent in
that table, or the cubic is limited near 20 bar.  Per the ruling the anchor is
not an inherited debt, so it is not replaced and the band is not widened.  Stopped:
the record 0.0289 stays.  A linear estimate from the two kij values bracketing
(not run) puts the kij that reproduces this point near 0.022, i.e. the record
number is closer to this isotherm than the global fit is.
