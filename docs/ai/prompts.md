# Prompt for your AI assistant

Paste this once, then write your task below it. Your assistant reads Choupo's instructions, prepares the case as a .zip, and you open it in the app with File, Open Case from .zip, run it, look, and go back to the assistant with what you saw.

## The prompt

```text
I want to work on a chemical process with Choupo (https://www.choupo.org), an open-source process simulator. Before anything else, learn it from its own instructions. Read these three whole, in this order:
1. https://www.choupo.org/llm/start-here.md
2. https://www.choupo.org/llm/brief.md (the contract: what a case is, how a stream is fixed, units and sources, what a refusal means)
3. https://www.choupo.org/llm/capabilities.md (what Choupo does and does not do)
Tell me in ten lines what you learned, then wait for my task. When I give it, read the packs it needs (the brief ends with their index) and say which documents and packs you read.
Rules. A tutorial is a worked check, never a template: derive my case from the contract, and do not open a shipped tutorial before your own case is written; say when you did. Never invent a property, price or parameter. Choupo's catalogue of components and binary pairs is small on purpose. If something is missing: I bring measured data; you prepare a fitting case; I open it in the app and run it; Choupo fits and shows how well; then the result goes into my case. If any other datum is missing, ask me.
Give me the case as a .zip, every value with its unit and source; first write the three decisions you took and why. If you could not run it, say what you expect to see, and name my steps in https://www.choupo.org/app: File, Open Case from .zip; File, Complete 0/; Run.
```
