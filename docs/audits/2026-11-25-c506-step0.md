## 3. Property calls the unit makes (and the gas route)

* Liquid: `liquidProperty::evaluate(thermo, Property::density|viscosity, ...)`
  (`Pipe.cpp` liquid path), the door; two-phase adds `thermo.density(T, P, y, DensityPhase::Vapour)`
  and `thermo.viscosityGas(T, y)` (`Pipe.cpp` two-phase path) for the gas.
* The gas line uses exactly those two vapour routes, never a literal and never the liquid door:
  `thermo.density(T, Pin, z, DensityPhase::Vapour)` (`ThermoPackage.H:931`; vapour molar volume from
  the equation of state, ideal gas included) and `thermo.viscosityGas(T, P, z)`
  (`ThermoPackage.H:774`; Wilke mixing of the declared transport model, e.g. Chung).  `c = P1/rho1`
  therefore carries the package's own Z; no gas constant is typed.  `check_liquid_property_ratchet`
  does not see the gas calls (they are not liquid properties).

## 4. The model and its window (details: Theory Guide `sec:pipe-gasline`)

`P1^2 - P2^2 = c G^2 [ f L/D + sum K + 2 ln(P1/P2) ]`; the mean-density shortcut is the same without
the log term, `P2^2 = P1^2 - c G^2 (f L/D + sum K)`.  Re = G D / mu is constant (mu depends on T only), so
one f.  Choking where dP/dL -> infinity: P = G sqrt(c) (isothermal Mach number 1; equivalently the
adiabatic Mach number 1/sqrt(gamma) -- gamma is not needed).  REFUSED by name: inlet at or beyond the
limit, line longer than L_max, dz != 0, no gas viscosity.  Advisory above a 40 % drop (a flag level, not
a derived bound).  Z held at its inlet value.  Adiabatic and Joule-Thomson effects are not modelled.
