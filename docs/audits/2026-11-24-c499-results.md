The method is offered to main ONLY if it needs fewer simulations than `sqp` to come within
1e-2 (relative) of the best known feasible value on the EXPENSIVE case, median over five
seeds.  Otherwise it stays on its branch with this table.

## Verdict by the rule

NOT OFFERED.  On the expensive case the Bayesian method needed 23 simulations in the median
against 22 for `sqp`: not fewer.  It stays on its branch.

## What the table also says (read, not part of the rule)

* On the cheap control the Bayesian method gets near the optimum faster (15 against 25) but
  rarely finishes it (n(1e-3) reached on 2 of 5 seeds): the expected shape of a surrogate
  method, which the protocol's advice (finish with `method sqp` from its best point) covers.
* On the column, 14 to 18 of its 25 simulations FAILED (the column does not converge, or its
  closure breaks the band and is counted as a failure, never a number).  The low-discrepancy
  start spends its budget in a region of the box where the column does not run; `sqp` from
  the case's own start fails 1-2 times.  A box drawn tighter after looking would change this,
  and that is exactly why the box was fixed before the runs.
* `sqp` from the start of seeds 1 and 5 either stalls far from the optimum (8985 kW) or
  fails at its first point: a single start is not robust on this column either.
* Central differences reach the cheap optimum to 1e-3 on every seed; forward differences
  (today's step of 1e-5) do not on two.  This is the same finding as the v1 bench.
