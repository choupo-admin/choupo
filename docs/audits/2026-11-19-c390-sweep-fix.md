### The defect it answers (g1)

With the lower limit of the pressure integral at psat(T) the limit MOVES with T,
so (dH/dT)_P = T (dS/dT)_P (Maxwell) is broken by the net term -v dpsat/dT:
7e-4 of Cp on water.  `check_flash_specification` (g1), which recovers T from
(P,H) and (P,S) on the same state, caught it at 1e-6.  With a fixed reference the
integrals share their lower limit and the identity is exact; (g1) passes at 1e-6.

### What the term is now

`Delta h_i = integral from P_ref,i to P of (v - T v_T) dP`, `Delta s_i = - integral of v_T dP`,
with `P_ref,i = psat_i(T_ref,i)`, `T_ref,i = min(298.15 K, 0.9 Tc_i)`; held at
psat(T) when P is below it (no pressure effect on a liquid below its own
saturation pressure).  Printed per component once per run:

    [thermo] liquid pressure term (water): v (1 - alpha T) dP from the reference
    pressure P_ref = psat(298.15 K) = 3.16982 kPa ...

For a component with a reference equation the leg is the equation's own
h(T,P) and s(T,P) on the liquid branch (metastable below psat), so the volume
route and the Helmholtz route agree on one number.

or ledger the leg can reach); 101 moved before the re-record, none after.

   recipe01 t_peak 770 -> 1087 s) moved WITH THE TERM OFF as well (measured:
   binary with the leg disabled gives the same -322.7 kJ against main's -291.3 kJ);
   restoring the bug on the branch reproduces main's -291.29 exactly.  A DEFECT,
   already fixed in 59833ba (the unit of `P_op_`), recorded here, goldens

3. ROUND-OFF ROWS.  Rows of a quantity that was exactly 0 and is now 1e-17 .. 1e-9
