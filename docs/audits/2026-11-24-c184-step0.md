### What the table says

* **Which solver runs which Newton.**  `WangHenke`, `totalReflux` and `shortcutColumn` run NO
  Newton (a bubble-point sweep, a stage march, closed forms), so the bounded/legacy question
  does not arise for them.  The simultaneous MESH (`model simultaneous`) and the full MESH
  (`model fullMESH`) call `solver::newtonND`; `model rateBased` calls it too
  (`RateBasedSegment.cpp:731`, Armijo, block-tridiagonal).
* **The default is the BOUNDED Armijo + Levenberg-Marquardt Newton** for every package:
  `newton` defaults to `bounded` (`:3253`), the box of the simultaneous MESH is set at `:3595`,
  the full MESH always sets `Globalisation::armijo` (`:4187`).
* **BRAIN.md's statement is stale for two of its three cases.**  `stripper01_sour_water` and
  `column13_sour_water_stage_identity` (aqueous chemistry) run the BOUNDED Newton and
  converge in 6 and 7 iterations as shipped; DEV.md 4c C245 slice 3 moved them
  (comment at `:3204-3212`).  Only `stripper02_sour_water_h2s` runs LEGACY, and it says so:
  `newton legacy;` is declared in its `flowsheetDict` (`:3253-3277`; the run prints the
  announcement, 36 iterations).
* **Phase 1 of every REACTIVE column is legacy**, by design and announced (`:3688-3705`):
  `column05_reactive_methylacetate`, `column19_mtbe_reactive_mesh` and
  `column20_mtbe_kinetic_mesh` (reaction off, products at x = 0, a degenerate face the
  bounded search stalls on).  Phase 2 and the full MESH are bounded.
* **No case in the corpus fails to converge**; nothing here exposes a defect of the Newton.
  The slowest column jobs are the two Klemola Wang-Henke cases (762 and 711 outer sweeps,
  32-40 s) and the rate-based beds (35-47 s), not a Newton.
