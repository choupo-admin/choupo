# Trademarks

This document records the trademark situation of the **Choupo**
project, states what anyone may do with the name and the logo without
asking, and clarifies how third-party trademarks are used in the
project's documentation.

## Choupo (the project name and marks)

"Choupo" is the project name.  The name, the logo and any associated
marks are owned by **TalentGround Lda.**, the founder's family holding
company.  The CHOUPO mark is **registered** with INPI (Portugal) under
**769805 MNA** (published 2026-06-16), in classes 9 and 42, and is used
with the "®" symbol.

The GNU GPL, version 3 (under which the code is released) explicitly
does **not** grant trademark rights, as no open-source licence does ---
the *code* is open, the *name* is owned.

**Why the split exists.**  The licence lets anyone use, study, change
and redistribute the code, and that freedom is the point of the
project.  The name does something the licence cannot: it tells a
student that what they are being handed is this project's work,
reviewed by the people named in `AUTHORS`.  A fork is free to be
better; it is not free to be mistaken for this one.

### What you may do without asking

- **Refer to Choupo by name** in articles, reviews, comparisons,
  citations, course materials, slides and similar descriptive
  contexts.
- **Teach with it, publish about it, and cite it.**  Use
  `CITATION.cff`.
- **Say truthfully what your work is.**  A fork or a downstream tool
  may state its relationship in plain descriptive language --- "based
  on Choupo", "a fork of Choupo", "compatible with Choupo case files",
  "reads Choupo dictionaries" --- provided the statement is accurate
  and the mark is not the prominent element of your own name or logo.
- **Reproduce the logo unaltered** to illustrate the project (a talk,
  a paper, a link, a course page).

### What needs permission

- Using "Choupo", or a confusingly similar name, **as the name** of a
  derivative work, fork, product or service --- anything that reads as
  endorsement by, or affiliation with, the project or the mark's
  owner.  If you fork Choupo, give the derivative its own name; the
  descriptive statement above is yours to use freely.
- **Altering the logo**, incorporating it into another mark, or using
  it as the brand of your own product.
- Using the mark on **merchandise**, or in a domain name.
  `choupo.org` and its subdomains are the project's.

Contact the maintainer (see `AUTHORS`) --- permission is usually a
short conversation, not a legal process.

### Writing the name

Three registers, and they are not interchangeable:

| Form | Where |
|---|---|
| `CHOUPO` | the visual product brand: logo, banner art, the app's wordmark, the homepage hero |
| `Choupo` | running prose --- README, docs, papers, comments, commit messages |
| `choupo` | code: binaries, the `Choupo::` namespace, `CHOUPO_HOME` and `CHOUPO_VERSION` |

**Choupo is a word, not an acronym.**  It is Portuguese for the
poplar, the tree drawn in every source-file header.  The six words
beside that tree --- Chemicals, Heat-transfer, Operations, Units,
Properties, Optimization --- are a decorative acrostic, not the origin
of the name and not an expansion of it.  Please do not write the name
as an initialism or expand it as one.

Note: the **copyright** on the source code remains with the authors
named in `AUTHORS`.  Trademark and copyright are distinct rights here
--- the *name* belongs to the holding, the *code* is authored under
GPL-3.0-or-later.

## Third-party names

The Choupo project is **independent** and is **not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or sponsored by** any third party.

Mentions of institutions and companies (e.g. Rice University, Peking
University, Universidade de Lisboa, Instituto Superior Técnico,
Petrobras, Chevron) are descriptive --- they cite the institutional
affiliations of co-authors or of industrial users.  All such marks
remain the property of their respective owners.

## Reporting concerns

If you believe any usage in this repository misrepresents your
trademark or violates your trademark guidelines, please contact the
project maintainer (see `AUTHORS`) so the wording can be reviewed.
