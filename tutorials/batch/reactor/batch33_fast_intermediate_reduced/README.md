# batch33_fast_intermediate_reduced — the quasi-steady intermediate

The reduced twin of `batch32_fast_intermediate_full`: one law, A -> P at
k1 c_A, written in `constant/reactions` as a `type expression` law.  The
intermediate is not carried; k2 does not appear.

## What to look at

1. **One rate constant in the stable species alone.**
2. **What is lost.**  The I in transit: 0.95 % of the charge at most, against
   the full twin.
