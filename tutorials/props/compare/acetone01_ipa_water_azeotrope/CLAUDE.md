## Intent (this case) -- keep this updated as the project develops

- **Goal:** the first RUNNABLE slice of the acetone-from-2-propanol reference
  work. One question only: *how close does a component Choupo has never
  curated, priced by a model that saw no data for it, land on a published
  azeotrope?*

- **The result, measured 2026-08-12** (1 atm, predictive UNIFAC, isopropanol
  from `data/groupEstimative/`):

  | | Luyben (2011) | Choupo | difference |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | water normal bp | 373.00 K | 372.45 K | **−0.55 K** |
  | IPA normal bp | 355.40 K | 359.91 K | **+4.51 K** |
  | T at Luyben's x\* = 0.6732 | 353.40 K | 355.99 K | +2.59 K |
  | azeotrope location x\* | 0.6732 | **0.5901** | −0.083 |

  T_bubble AAD over the three points: **2.55 K (0.72 %)**.

- **What the numbers say, and the split is the lesson.** The two error sources
  separate cleanly:
  - **Water is curated** and lands within 0.55 K. That leg is the control.
  - **The +4.51 K on pure IPA is not UNIFAC at all.** At a pure composition
    UNIFAC returns γ = 1 by construction, so the whole error is the
    *lake-estimated vapour pressure*: the record's Joback `Tb` is 359.98 K
    against a measured 355.4 K. An estimated component costs ~4.5 K on its own
    boiling point before any mixture model is consulted.
  - **UNIFAC gets the azeotrope's EXISTENCE right and its PLACE wrong** —
    0.590 against 0.673, off by 0.083 in mole fraction. That is the documented
    weakness of original UNIFAC on alcohol–water, and it is the same direction
    and roughly the same size as the misplacement seen in
    `compare_vle_etoh_water`.
  - Most of the +2.59 K at Luyben's x\* is *inherited* from the IPA boiling
    point, not generated by the mixture model. A reader who quotes the 2.59 K
    as "UNIFAC's error" has attributed to the mixture model an error the pure
    component brought with it.

- **Decisions + why:**
  - **UNIFAC, not UNIQUAC**, although the paper runs UNIQUAC: Choupo ships no
    UNIQUAC pair for IPA–water, and inventing one would convert *unsourced*
    into *falsely sourced*. UNIFAC needs no pair.
  - **isopropanol lives in this case's `constant/components/`**, copied from
    the lake. It is deliberately NOT promoted to `data/standards/` — that is a
    curation act, and curation is reserved.
  - The three published points are pinned as **self-recorded `aad` rows, never
    `anchor` rows**: Luyben's azeotrope is his UNIQUAC model's value and the
    AAD is derived from it and this model together, so no primary published it.
    What the suite holds is that the agreement has not MOVED.

### What measuring the water leg found (2026-08-12)

The three bubble points are published as operation diagnostics now, and the
one that was meant to be the boring control turned out not to be:

| | Choupo | reference | |
|---|---|---|---|
| water normal bp | **372.4536 K** | 373.15 K (definition) | **−0.70 K** |
| IPA normal bp | 359.9075 K | 355.40 K (literature) | +4.51 K |
| T at Luyben's x\* | 355.9854 K | 353.40 K (his model) | +2.59 K |

`bp_water` was the one point here that could honestly have carried an `anchor`
row — the normal boiling point of water is fixed by definition, not measured —
and asking for it is what found that **Choupo's own curated water record does
not reproduce it**. `data/standards/components/water.dat` carries the Antoine
set (5.40221, 1838.675, −31.737) over `Trange (273 373)`; solved for 1 atm
that gives 372.4536 K, and at 373.15 K it returns 1.0393 bar — 2.6 % high.

The diagnosis is not that the record is wrong but that it is being **used at
the extreme top of its own declared window**, where this NIST set is known to
be weak (its companion set covers 379–573 K). Every corpus case that boils
water at 1 atm inherits the 0.70 K.

Not absorbed here behind a loose band, and not fixed: re-curating water's
vapour pressure is a curation act (reserved) and would move a large number of
goldens. Recorded, with the arithmetic, so the decision can be made on
evidence.
