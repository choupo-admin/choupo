## Intent (this case) -- keep this updated as the project develops

- **Goal:** run column C2 of Luyben's Figure 1 (19 stages, feed 16, RR 0.849,
  distillate 5.88 kmol/h, 1 atm) and find out what quality of **isopropanol
  recycle** it can return, and what that costs the **water product**.

- **The prediction it tests**, committed to the repository the day before this
  case was written (`acetone01`, commit *"The prediction for C2, written before
  C2 exists"*):

  > "C2's overhead should cap at roughly x_IPA 0.59 and cannot reach his
  > 0.650. The gap is 0.06 in mole fraction, about 9 % relative, and it is a
  > HARD ceiling rather than a shortfall in stages or reflux."

### What it found, measured 2026-08-13

**1. THE PREDICTION HOLDS, and it is tighter than it was written.**

Rigorous MESH, **7 Newton iterations, ‖F‖ = 1.77e-11**:

> **x_D(isopropanol) = 0.57975**, against Luyben's **0.6500**.

The predicted ceiling was "roughly 0.59" — `acetone01`'s measured azeotrope is
at **0.5901**, and the column tops out **just below it**, pinched against it
exactly as C1 is pinched against its own. The gap to the paper is **−10.8 %
relative**, and it is a ceiling: no reflux or stage count reaches 0.65,
because 0.65 is on the far side of a minimum-boiling azeotrope.

**2. THE COST LANDS ON THE WATER PRODUCT, AND IT IS THE PLANT'S RAW MATERIAL.**

The distillate rate is Luyben's, 5.88 kmol/h. Isopropanol that the ceiling
keeps out of the recycle has nowhere to go but down:

| | Choupo (UNIFAC) | Luyben (UNIQUAC) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| recycle, x_isopropanol | **0.57975** | 0.6500 | **−10.8 %** |
| recycle, x_water | 0.4196 | 0.3493 | +20 % |
| water product, x_water | **0.98707** | 0.999 | −1.2 % |
| water product, x_isopropanol | **0.01293** | — (he reports A 0.001 / W 0.999) | |
| water product, kmol/h | 34.680 | 34.67 | +0.03 % |
| T top (K) | 355.88 | 353.4 (his azeotrope T) | +2.5 K |
| T bottom (K) | **366.17** | 377 | **−10.8 K** |
| condenser duty (kW) | −120.94 | 119.3 | **+1.4 %** |
| reboiler duty (kW) | 114.90 | 120.4 | **−4.6 %** |

Of the 3.857 kmol/h of isopropanol entering C2, **0.448 kmol/h leaves in the
water product — 11.6 %**. Luyben loses 0.9 %. In the closed plant that is not
a purity nuisance, it is **raw material walking out of the bottom of the
column**, and it will show up in acetone08 as a fresh-feed requirement the
paper does not have.

The +2.5 K at the top is not an independent error: the overhead sits at the
azeotrope, and `acetone01` already measured that this model's azeotrope
temperature is +2.59 K high, most of it *inherited from the lake-estimated
isopropanol boiling point* rather than generated by UNIFAC. The −10.8 K at the
bottom is the 1.29 % isopropanol that should not be there depressing the
bubble point.

**3. THE DUTIES ARE INSIDE 5 %, AGAIN.** Condenser +1.4 %, reboiler −4.6 %.
That is now the fourth unit of this flowsheet where the energy survives and
the separation does not — reactor 87.5 %, HX1 +7.6 %, C1 condenser −4.5 %, and
now both of C2's. **The pattern is the result**, and it has a physical reason:
a duty is an integral over the whole stream and is dominated by sensible and
latent heat that the pure-component records carry well, while a separation is
decided by a *ratio of activity coefficients near a singularity*, where a small
model error is amplified without limit.

**4. THE TWO COLUMNS FAIL IN OPPOSITE WAYS, and reporting them as one number
would destroy the finding.**

* **C1**: UNIFAC *invents* an azeotrope in acetone/water where Luyben's
  UNIQUAC has none. The column loses purity the paper does not lose. The
  remedy is a fitted pair — or the discovery that the azeotrope is real.
* **C2**: the IPA/water azeotrope is **real and both models have it**. UNIFAC
  only puts it in the wrong *place* (0.590 vs 0.673). The column would fail to
  reach 0.65 even with a perfect column, and so would the real plant if the
  azeotrope were where UNIFAC says.

A reader who averages these into "UNIFAC is ~10 % off" has thrown away the
difference between a spurious feature and a misplaced real one. They need
different fixes and they carry different risk.

### Decisions + why

- **`model simultaneous`.** Same reason as C1: the corpus default is
  documented as unstable through an azeotrope and an azeotrope is the feature
  under test.
- **Luyben's published B1 as the feed, not `acetone06`'s answer.** This case
  isolates the column. `acetone06`'s real bottoms carries 2.14 mol % acetone
  against his 0.01 %, and feeding it here would test two columns at once.
  `acetone08` wires the real streams; the difference between this case and
  that one *is* the propagation the plant exists to show.
- **His distillate rate, 5.88 kmol/h, held fixed.** That is what makes the
  ceiling visible as a *wetter recycle at the same flow* rather than as a
  smaller one. The plant's isopropanol inventory has to go somewhere, and the
  honest question is what quality it comes back at.
- **No `anchor` rows**, for the third time and the same reason: every number
  in his table is his Aspen/UNIQUAC output, not a measurement. The golden's
  claim is regression.

### Announced along the way

* water's vapour pressure at 373.9 K, just outside its declared 273–373
  window — the catalogue limit `acetone01`/`acetone04` measure as a 0.70 K
  deficit at the normal boiling point;
* acetone's vapour pressure at 242.7 K, below its declared window. Acetone is
  present at 10⁻⁴ here and the request comes from a bubble-point bracket
  reaching, not from a state the column occupies — announced rather than
  clipped.
