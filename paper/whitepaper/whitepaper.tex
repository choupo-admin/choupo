\begin{abstract}
\noindent
A language model can now write a plausible five-hundred-page process design
in minutes.  Plausible is not verified, and a student handed such a design
has no way to tell which of its numbers were computed and which were
remembered.  We argue that the value of a process simulator in teaching is
moving from \emph{producing} a design to \emph{proving} one, and that an AI
agent should not compute engineering answers from memory but call a
deterministic, open and auditable tool and show what it did.  We describe
Choupo, an open-source (GPL-3.0-or-later), dependency-free C++17 process
simulator built around that idea: it refuses by name rather than default in
silence, closes mass, atoms and energy on every converged steady run by
default, carries a source and a licence for every curated value, and keeps a
small named subset of cases anchored to published measurements.  Its
development is organised so that each class of error, once found, gets a
gate that makes it unable to leave in silence.  We compare it on a published
sample case with an open-source simulator, describe how a student works with
an agent around it, and state plainly what it does not do.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem: plausible is not verified}
\label{sec:problem}

An engineering student today can ask an AI agent for the design of a
distillation train, an ammonia loop or a crystalliser, and receive within
minutes a document that has every outward mark of competence: a flowsheet,
stream tables, duties, equipment sizes, a cost estimate, a page of
assumptions.  Some of its numbers will be right.  Some will be the kind of
error a reviewer spends a career learning to spot: an energy balance that
does not close, a vapour pressure extrapolated eighty kelvin past its fit, a
heat of reaction with the wrong reference state, a column fed a liquid that
is in fact two-phase, a cost on a volume computed in kilomoles.  None of
these errors announces itself.  A language model produces text that is
statistically likely; an energy balance is not a matter of likelihood.

For a course this changes the question.  Producing a design is becoming
cheap.  What remains expensive, and what a graduate will be paid for, is
knowing \emph{which numbers in a design can be trusted, and how one would
know}.  A design that cannot be checked is not worth much more than the
time it took to generate, however long it is.

The usual answer is ``run it through a simulator''.  But most simulators a
student meets were built for a different reader: an expert who already knows
what the program does when a parameter is missing, a model is used out of
range, or an iteration stops short.  The student sees converged numbers.
Whether a missing interaction parameter was silently set to zero, whether a
recycle converged or merely stopped, whether the enthalpy reference of the
reactor agrees with that of the stream table, is not on the screen.  Handing
an agent such a tool moves the opacity from the agent to the tool; it does
not remove it.

This paper describes a tool designed for the other reader: one who must be
able to check every answer, and who increasingly works through an agent.

\section{The thesis: the agent calls the tool, the tool shows its work}
\label{sec:thesis}

Our thesis has two halves.

\emph{First, an agent should not compute engineering answers from memory.}
It should call a deterministic, open and auditable program and report what
that program did: which models it assembled, which data it read and from
where, which iterations it took, which balances closed and which did not.
The agent's strengths --- reading documentation, drafting case files, fitting
a parameter, explaining an equation --- are real, and they are best used to
\emph{prepare} a computation that something else performs and checks.

\emph{Second, the value of a design moves from who wrote it to what proves it
right.}  If an agent writes the case files and the simulator solves them,
the student's contribution is no longer typing; it is judgement.  That
judgement needs evidence on the screen: a refusal that names what is missing,
a conservation report that says how far a plant is from closing, a caveat
that says which property was extrapolated.  A simulator built for this reader
must therefore be \emph{glass-box} in a strong sense: not only open source,
but explicit at run time about every assumption it makes.

Choupo is itself developed under this thesis.  Substantial parts of its
code, documentation and tutorial corpus were drafted by AI agents working
under written briefs, reviewed and curated by the human maintainers; a change
is integrated only after the validation its reach requires has passed, and
the engine's checks, not the author's confidence, decide whether an answer
has moved.
% CLAUDE.md sec 1 (provenance: assistance under human curation); sec 0 rule 4
% and sec 10 "VALIDATION IS SIZED BY WHAT THE CHANGE CAN REACH" and "a commit
% lands only on FAIL 0 of the validation its slice requires"; DEV.md:9870
% (the command doctrine: one commander, written briefs to generals).
The rest of this paper argues that this is a workable arrangement only
because the checks were designed first.

\section{How Choupo works}
\label{sec:architecture}

Choupo is a steady-state and dynamic process simulator written in C++17 with
no external libraries: the Newton solvers, the linear algebra, the
integrators, the optimisers and the dictionary parser are all hand-written,
readable, and part of the source a student can open.
% CLAUDE.md sec 2 ("Zero external dependencies -- only a C++17 compiler and
% GNU make") and sec 10 "No external libs" (CLAUDE.md:3836); src/solver/
% (NewtonRaphson, NewtonND, LU, Wegstein, NelderMead, SQP, ActiveSetQP,
% StabilityTest, ODE/).

\subsection{Three layers}

The engine has three layers (Figure~\ref{fig:arch}).  The \emph{simulator
core} turns one flowsheet into one solution --- streams, unit results and key
performance indicators --- as a pure function of its inputs.  An optional
\emph{outer driver} calls that function repeatedly: parameter sweeps, design
specifications, optimisation, parameter estimation.  An optional
\emph{post-processor} chain augments a converged solution with equipment
sizing, costing and reports.
% CLAUDE.md:399 (sec 4, "Architecture: 3 layers"); the pure function is
% runSimulation, src/applications/choupoSolve/main.cpp:145, called by the
% direct path (:1222) and by every outer driver's functor (:894);
% src/outerDriver/OuterDriver.H:73; src/postProcessing/PostProcessor.H:67.
Five binaries partition the problem classes:
\code{choupoSolve} (steady state, $F(x)=0$, with recycles solved by Newton on
the tear streams), \code{choupoBatch} (batch campaigns driven by a recipe),
\code{choupoSemiContinuous} (a time-integrated flowsheet without control
loops: start-up, fed-batch, feed-and-bleed), \code{choupoCtrl} (the same
integration with a control layer) and \code{choupoProps} (a property
workbench: evaluations, consistency tests, parameter fitting).
% ls src/applications/ -> choupoBatch choupoCtrl choupoProps
% choupoSemiContinuous choupoSolve; CLAUDE.md:3866 (the fifth class, ruled
% 2026-09-20); src/dynamicDriver/ is the one home both dynamic binaries
% share.  generated/releaseInventory.json (c402561ad): "engines": 5.
Numerical strategies coexist inside each binary and are selected in the case
files; there is no binary per algorithm.

\subsection{The case is a directory of plain-text dictionaries}

A case is a directory.  \code{system/} says \emph{how} (control,
flowsheet topology, solver options, the outer driver, post-processing),
\code{constant/} says \emph{with what} (the thermophysical system, reactions,
case-local data), and \code{0/} holds the complete initial state, one file per
stream.  The flowsheet dictionary carries topology only; a stream's values
live in its own file, and a missing or orphan stream file is a fatal error.

\paragraph{Explicit factories.}  Every extensible base class --- unit
operations, activity models, equations of state, outer drivers,
post-processors and the rest --- has a factory, and every built-in type is
registered by one line inside a \code{registerBuiltins()} function that
\code{main()} calls explicitly.  There are no self-registration macros and
no static initialisers, so ``where does this model come from?'' is answered
by reading one function.
% CLAUDE.md:495 (sec 5 "Factory pattern -- always explicit"); the calls in
% src/applications/choupoSolve/main.cpp:443-468; the registration line for
% the flash, src/unitOperations/UnitOperation.cpp:144 and :157; the factory
% signature src/unitOperations/UnitOperation.H:228-237.

\paragraph{State directories are restartable snapshots.}  The steady solution
is written to \code{converged/} in the same layout as \code{0/}, and only
when the run converged: the directory's name is a contract.  Each unit's
internal state (a column's stage profile, a reactor's axial profile, a
crystal size distribution) is written beside the streams under
\code{internalStates/}, so a converged directory renamed \code{0/} restarts
the case.  The time-integrated binaries write a time directory at every
write interval in the same layout.  \code{iterations/} keeps the numerical
history when asked for, never physical time.

\paragraph{What Choupo takes from OpenFOAM, and where it departs.}
Choupo's case organisation is inspired by OpenFOAM \citep{weller1998}, an
open-source (GPL) C++ toolbox for computational fluid dynamics whose
transparent, plain-text dictionaries we regard as one of the most elegant
designs in scientific software.  From it Choupo takes: hierarchical
plain-text dictionaries; the case directory as the unit of work, with
\code{system/}, \code{constant/}, \code{0/} and state or time directories;
one application per problem class; utilities as small programs in
\code{bin/}; and a release model in which the default branch is the
development line and each release is an immutable versioned tag.  Where an
analogy is used it is stated with its limits: Choupo's normalised residual
follows OpenFOAM's normalisation factor with equations in place of cells,
and its source states three reasons why it is not the same theorem.
% CLAUDE.md sec 2 "Branches & tags" (CLAUDE.md:170: the default branch IS
% the development line, a release is an immutable tag; the OpenFOAM-dev
% comparison is written there); sec 3, sec 4, sec 5 dictionaries;
% src/solver/Convergence.H:84-105 ("It is NOT the same theorem.  Three
% honest limits"); docs/design/normalized-residual-convergence.md.
It departs deliberately in three places.  Model selection uses the explicit
\code{registerBuiltins()} factories above instead of run-time-selection
macros; the domain is process simulation --- streams, unit operations,
phase equilibria --- not continuum mechanics; and there are no external
dependencies at all.
% CLAUDE.md:495 (no auto-registration via macros/static initialisers) and
% CLAUDE.md:3860; NOTICE:128-140 (the provenance review: dictionary parser,
% factories, Rosenbrock23 integrator and time-directory writer are
% independent implementations; no OpenFOAM source code adapted or reused).
\textbf{Choupo is an independent project.  It is not part of, affiliated
with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the OpenFOAM Foundation or OpenCFD Ltd},
and no OpenFOAM source code was adapted or reused in it.

\section{What makes an answer verifiable}
\label{sec:verifiable}

``Open source'' is necessary and far from sufficient.  A program whose
source can be read but whose run does not say what it assumed is checkable
only by someone prepared to read the source for every run.  Six properties
make a Choupo answer checkable by a student, and by the agent working with
one.

\paragraph{Refusal by name instead of a silent default.}  When a case is
missing something a model needs, or declares a word the engine does not
know, the run stops and says what is missing and what would fix it, naming the valid alternatives and, where one is close, the nearest.  A model name with a typing slip does not fall
through to a default model; a binary pair used outside the temperature span of
its evidence is announced; an approximation the author did not authorise is
refused, and one the author did authorise is printed above the results as a
divergence between the problem posed and the problem solved.
% src/core/RegistryRefusal.H:25-56 (the one home of a refusal by name; the
% closest-name suggestion; the if-chain whose else was a catch-all);
% gate check_model_default_registered (claim in generated/gateManifest.json,
% 25941a479); src/thermo/PairAudit.H (the [pair-span] / [unreviewed]
% announcements, :88); gate check_problem_divergence and
% docs/design/problem-divergence-contract.md.
The same rule binds the solver's own aids: an initial guess, a tear estimate
or a bound is declared in the case, and the solver reports aloud when it
uses one.
% CLAUDE.md:3909 ("No silent crutch", decided 2026-05-30).

\paragraph{Conservation, owned by the engine and on by default.}  Every
converged steady run writes three balances --- total mass, atoms per element
and energy --- whether or not the case asked for them, because conservation is
the curriculum.  A case may opt out explicitly; it cannot opt out by
forgetting.  Where an energy balance cannot be priced (a species without a
formation enthalpy), the default report says so and names the missing datum
rather than printing a zero.  The browser draws these balances; it never
recomputes them.
% src/applications/choupoSolve/main.cpp:940-1014 ("THE THREE BALANCES ARE
% DEFAULT DIAGNOSTICS of every converged steady run"; the elementBalance
% default at :1012-1014); gate check_default_reports (claim in
% gateManifest); docs/design/a-first-law-the-gui-computed-for-itself.md.
These balances matter because a regression test cannot see what they see.
A golden-master test pins what a run prints; a plant that loses two percent
of its energy, stably, passes its golden for ever.  Only a gate that reads
the balance and refuses can catch it (Section~\ref{sec:gates}).
% CLAUDE.md:1008 ("A SUITE'S PASS COVERS WHAT ITS GATES ASSERT, AND NOTHING
% ELSE"); docs/design/what-a-passing-suite-does-not-say.md.

\paragraph{A source and a licence for every value.}  The curated catalogue
cites the primary source per value, never the aggregator's arrangement;
each record says where its numbers came from and how they were obtained
(measured, regressed, estimated), and a record that has not been reviewed,
or comes from a private local tier, is announced as such when a run reads
it.  A dataset with no licence statement, or a NonCommercial one, is kept
out of the public tree, and a pair table does not acquire its courier's
licence by being redistributed inside another package.  The engine refuses
to write into the curated catalogue: new data enters it by a human act of
curation.

\paragraph{A named validation subset.}  Most of Choupo's regression corpus
compares each case with an answer an earlier Choupo run recorded.  A pass
proves the answer has not moved; it says nothing about whether it was ever
right.  The project therefore separates \emph{verification} from
\emph{validation} \citep{nasa7009} and keeps a small, named subset of cases
anchored to independent published results: the Williams--Otto reactor and
its published steady state \citep{williams1960}, the IAPWS-IF97 verification
tables \citep{wagner2000}, the Hamer--Wu activity coefficients of uni-univalent
salts \citep{hamer1972}, a measured calcite pH \citep{plummer1982}, and
others.  Where an anchor is the model's own training data the case says so
and is not counted as validation.

The subset is small, and Section~\ref{sec:limits} says so.

\paragraph{A check that cannot run must not pass.}  A gate whose input has
disappeared, or whose tool is not installed, or whose harness died, must
refuse, never report success; and a tool that could not look must not
report what it did not see.  This sounds obvious and is the property most
easily lost, because a permanently green check looks exactly like a healthy
one.  Each gate is verified by sabotage: the defect it exists to catch is
introduced on purpose and the gate is observed to fail.

\paragraph{A re-recorded answer is a claim.}  When an engine change moves a

numerical reason it moved, and shown to the maintainer before anything is
re-recorded.  A row that moves for a reason nobody can state is treated as a

\section{How a class of error becomes unable to leave in silence}

users before the check that now catches them existed.  What distinguishes a
verifiable tool is not the absence of errors but what its architecture does

\section{Pedagogy: auditing a design is the competence}
\label{sec:pedagogy}

A simulation course has traditionally taught students to \emph{set up} a
simulation: choose a property method, specify a column, converge a recycle.
When an agent can do the setting up, the competence that remains is
\emph{auditing}: reading a design and finding where it is wrong.  That is
exactly what a glass-box simulator exercises.  A student who sees that a
vapour pressure was extrapolated asks whether the answer depends on it; a
student who sees a plant's first law miss by three percent looks for the
unit that carries the gap; a student who sees a refusal learns what the
model needed.

Choupo supports this in three ways.  The source is meant to be read: small
classes, explicit factories, hand-written numerics.  The run log shows the
computation at the level of detail a lecture would: the $K$-values, the
Rachford--Rice iterations, the composition loop.  And a set of
\emph{EduTools} --- short, interactive pages in the browser, each built
around one idea and running the real engine where a computation is
involved --- covers ten disciplines from thermodynamics and separations to
process safety and economics; the site's generated catalogue lists 61 of
them live.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

Nothing in this paper should be read as a claim of breadth.  Choupo does not
aim at parity with industrial simulators: its component catalogue is curated
rather than encyclopaedic, and it has no equation-oriented solver; its
models cover what its tutorials exercise.
% CLAUDE.md sec 1 ("not aiming for breadth or thermo-curation parity");
% sec 10 (the equation-oriented dream deferred);
% generated/releaseInventory.json (c402561ad) for the current counts
% (components 604; unit operations 52; runnable cases 480).

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{A regression pass is not validation.}  Most of the corpus is
  compared with answers Choupo itself recorded; a pass proves the answer has
  not moved, not that it is right.  The validation subset anchored to
  published measurements is small, and only some of its claims are checked
  by machine rather than stated in prose.
  % docs/architecture/verification-and-validation.md sec 1 and sec 3 ("Every
  % other row here is still a claim in prose").
\item \emph{Open first-law debts are pinned, not closed.}  The energy gate
  holds named plants that do not yet close their first law within one
  percent; they are listed with their residuals rather than hidden, and they
  are open.
  % gate check_energy_closure claim (25941a479): 9 cases PINNED in KNOWN_OPEN
  % and 8 in KNOWN_OPEN_KW, "ledgers of plants that do NOT conserve energy".
\item \emph{Some balances have not been measured.}  The first law of the
  time-integrated batch reactor and dynamic CSTR has not been measured by
  the slice that closed it for their steady counterparts, and is claimed
  neither way.
  % CLAUDE.md sec 6 (C77: "the time-integrated batchReactor and dynamicCSTR
  % were NOT measured by that slice and are not claimed either way").
\item \emph{A gate's reach is its scope.}  The benchmark cases are outside
  the regression suite, and a gate sees only the cases it walks.  The
  stage-1 feed of Section~\ref{sec:comparison} was flagged by the default
  report but not refused, because no tutorial exercised it.
  % benchmarks/README.md (not in the regression suite; no gate holds the
  % comparison); case B README C1 (33e277ca2).
\item \emph{Known numerical limits} are documented beside the feature they
  bound: bubble-point distillation through an azeotrope is unstable and the
  simultaneous method is the documented escape; the liquid--liquid flash of
  Section~\ref{sec:comparison} is being corrected.
  % CLAUDE.md sec 9 (Known limitations); case C README (33e277ca2).
\item \emph{The pedagogical claim is untested.}  We have argued that auditing
  an agent's design is the competence a simulation course should teach; we
  have not measured whether students learn it better this way.
\end{itemize}
